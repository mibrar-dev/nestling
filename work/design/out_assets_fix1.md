**Not changed, deliberately:** `app_icon_foreground.svg` stays at **66%** (`scale(0.88)`). 66% is Android's guaranteed-visible adaptive-icon safe area — a platform constraint, not a style choice — so the composite and the adaptive foreground now differ by 4%.
